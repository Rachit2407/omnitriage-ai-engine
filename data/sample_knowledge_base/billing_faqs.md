# Billing Cycles, Proration & Seat Management

## Adding Seats Mid-Cycle
Adding new team members or administrative seats mid-cycle triggers immediate access provisioning. Charges are prorated to the exact remaining seconds in the current billing period and billed on the subsequent scheduled monthly invoice.

## Seat Downgrades & Removals
When an administrative seat is revoked or downgraded, the change takes effect immediately. The remaining unused value of that seat for the current cycle is credited back to the organization's account balance as an un-expiring billing credit.

## Cash Refund Policy
OmniTriage operates under an automated credit-based adjustment model. Cash refunds back to credit cards or bank accounts for downgraded or unused seats are NOT provided under standard Terms of Service, unless required by local statutory consumer protection laws. Credits automatically offset future recurring invoices.
